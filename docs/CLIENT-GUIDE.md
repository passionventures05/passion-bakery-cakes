# Managing your cakes (simple guide)

Open **your-website.com/admin** and press **Login with GitHub**. Click **Cakes** on the left.

## Add a new cake
1. Press **＋ Cake**.
2. **Cake name:** the name customers see. Every cake needs a different name.
3. **Photo:** press *Choose an image* → *Upload* → pick the photo → *Choose selected*.
   - JPG or WebP, at least 800 px wide, **under 800 KB** (large phone photos slow the site: shrink them first
     at squoosh.app).
4. **Category:** pick one from the list.
5. **Starting price (₹)** and **Base size:** the price is for the base size. Other sizes are priced in
   proportion (a 1 kg cake costs double the 500 g price).
6. **Sizes customers can choose:** tick every size you sell. Use *either* weights (cakes) *or* pieces
   (jars, cupcakes), not both.
7. **Description:** a sentence or two about the flavour and toppings.
8. Press **Publish → Publish now**.

The cake appears on the website in **1–2 minutes**.

## Change a price, photo or description
Click the cake in the list, change it, then **Publish → Publish now**.

## Hide a cake (out of season) without deleting it
Open the cake, switch **Show on website** off, publish. Switch it back on any time.

## Delete a cake
Open it → **Delete entry**. (Hiding is safer: you can bring it back.)

## Where does the cake appear in the list?
New cakes go to the end. To place one earlier, fill **Position in the list**: a smaller number is shown
first (1 = first). The existing cakes are numbered 10, 20, 30… so, for example, 15 puts a cake between the
cakes at 10 and 20.

## Good to know
- Prices are whole rupees.
- Search the list with the box at the top; filter *No photo yet* or *Hidden cakes* from **Filter by**.
- If a change hasn't appeared after 5 minutes, tell your developer: the system may have found a mistake
  in a cake and kept the old website in place so nothing breaks.
